Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Your financial plan stays in local device or browser storage. Debtonate does not automatically send your financial entries to the developer.

Information you enter

Debtonate stores the financial information you enter, including account names, debt balances, interest rates, credit limits, payment history, bills, paycheck schedules, income, assets, liabilities, and payoff estimates. It also stores app preferences. No account, bank connection, Social Security number, bank password, or credit report is required. Please avoid entering sensitive identifiers in names or notes.

Collection and sharing

The current app has no developer-operated server, cloud sync, advertising, tracking, or analytics. Debtonate does not automatically upload your financial entries to Anthony Cole, credit bureaus, or lenders, and does not sell your financial information. Your entries are used locally to provide budgeting, payment tracking, and educational estimates.

Local storage and security

On iOS and Android, saved plans use platform-protected encrypted storage. Web plans use unencrypted browser storage. Device and browser backups may retain copies or store them elsewhere under your settings. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser may be able to view your information. Optional biometric app lock uses the operating system’s authentication services; Debtonate does not receive or store your face or fingerprint data. Security also depends on your device, passcode, and operating system.

Home Screen widgets

iPhone widgets display aggregate debt progress, paycheck remainders, unpaid bill amounts, and dates, even when app lock is enabled. Summaries are stored in a local shared App Group file protected by iOS file protection. They do not include account names, bill names, or income-source labels. Remove widgets if you do not want these summaries visible. Deleting your plan clears the shared summary, although iOS may temporarily retain a rendered widget.

Notifications

Optional payment and credit-report reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. You can disable them in the app or your device settings. The app does not make payments or verify that lender payments have occurred. Notification delivery depends on operating-system settings.

Backups and support requests

Manual exports are readable, unencrypted JSON files containing your financial records. You choose where to save or share them; your selected storage or sharing provider may receive the files. Store backups privately. Restoring a backup replaces your current plan. Problem reports contain the details you enter, app version, and platform, without an automatic financial attachment. You review them before using email or sharing. If you contact support, Anthony Cole receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid including financial records or other sensitive information in support messages.

Retention and deletion

Your plan remains locally until you change it, delete it, or clear its storage. Use Resources → Privacy & disclosures → Delete saved plan to clear saved financial records and reset your plan. Start over in the menu also resets appearance. On iOS, Keychain data may survive uninstalling, so delete your saved plan before uninstalling if you want to remove it. Deletion does not remove previously exported files, device or browser backups, or information held by external services. Delete those copies separately. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and maintain necessary records; contact the address below to request deletion of correspondence you provided.

External websites and platform services

Resource links open external websites, including CFPB, FTC, AnnualCreditReport.com, and credit bureaus. Debtonate does not retrieve credit reports or submit disputes. These sites and your operating system, app store, email, and backup providers have their own privacy practices. Debtonate is not affiliated with or endorsed by the linked organizations.
